Library model photos for the realistic pictures: 24 invented people from src/data.js CAST.
Two photos per person: <id>.jpg (full length, 3:4, facing the camera, plain light studio background, simple fitted
neutral clothing) and <id>-face.jpg (head-and-shoulders portrait of exactly the same person).
Made by Pink with: node scripts/make-models.mjs --dry, then without --dry (about $7.20 for all 24),
or by the founder by hand (docs/make-assets-guide.md) and added with scripts/add-assets.mjs.
Never a photo of a real or famous person. The app only shows the photos that exist.
